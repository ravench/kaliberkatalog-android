Kaliberkatalog is a reference for cartridges: over 700 calibres with their dimensions, a dimensioned drawing and the sources, free and without an internet connection.

ALL CALIBRES
Over 700 calibres, from the dawn of cartridge ammunition to today: rimfire, handgun, rifle, black powder, shotgun and air gun, including Swiss service rounds (GP 11, GP 90, GP 1890) and blanks. Search for calibres by name or with words like “blank” or “shotshell”.

FOR EACH CALIBRE
Its dimensions (depending on the type: overall length, case length, rim, base, shoulder, neck and bullet diameter) with their sources, a dimensioned drawing and the case head seen from below; where they exist, also the primer, the origin and other names. Some military calibres list the colour codes of the bullet tip, case base or primer ring. Rare and obsolete calibres are marked.

LOOKALIKES
Calibres that are easily mistaken for each other are grouped together, and the group says what tells them apart.

Dimensions are rounded reference values for identification, not reloading data. Matching dimensions do not mean a cartridge is safe to fire: check the headstamp and the calibre marked on the firearm. Kaliberkatalog does not sell or advertise firearms or ammunition.
